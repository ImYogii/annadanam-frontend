How this works

Annadanam means free food offered by temples, families, or community events. This site lists every Annadanam happening in and around the village.

Use the Today, Tomorrow, and Upcoming tabs to see what's on. Each listing shows the timing, venue, and a map — tap Navigate with Google Maps to get directions straight there.

Organizers can log in via Admin Login to post their own events.
